Seats, consoles, roofs and shades.

Open viewer → · Download Blender files and references

The original detailed Caymas is retained as the finish benchmark. Eleven other fixed-helm boats are rebuilt here; the bare Tracker utility boat retains its bench/tiller layout. Each reference pair uses the same saved boat listing. Sun Tracker and Princess shades are folded as photographed; Bennington has an open Bimini. These are photo-guided custom models, not OEM CAD: hidden construction and small dimensions remain approximate. Native close-ups show the structure; Garmin screens and selected motors are installed in the viewer.
